About The Event

“Deja View” is an inter-college web development competition where participants recreate a given website within a time limit, focusing on design accuracy, code quality, and creative enhancements. It emphasizes frontend skills, user experience, and innovative thinking in a practical, high-pressure setting.

Pixel-Perfect Recreation

Showcase your precision and attention to detail by recreating the given website as accurately as possible, capturing its layout, style, and structure.

Creative Twists

Go beyond imitation by adding your own innovative flair — enhance user interaction, improve layout, or introduce subtle animations that elevate the user experience.

Competitive Spirit

Compete with top developers across colleges as your submissions are judged on creativity, code quality, responsiveness, and user-centric design.

Rules & Guidelines

  • Each team can have a minimum of 1 and maximum of 2 participants.
  • Participants must bring their own laptops and chargers.
  • Internet access will be provided during the event.
  • The reference website will be displayed only once, so pay close attention.
  • Teams will be judged on accuracy, creativity, code quality, responsiveness, and user interface.
  • Use of any frameworks or libraries (like Bootstrap, Tailwind, etc.) is allowed.
  • Plagiarism or copying from other teams is strictly prohibited and may lead to disqualification.
  • Decisions made by the judging panel will be final and binding.
  • Judging Criteria: Design & Aesthetics 30%, Functionality 30%, Innovation & Creativity 20%, Code Quality & Structure 10% Responsiveness/User Experience 10%
