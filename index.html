About The Event

"Deja View" is a creative observation and memory-powered event where teams use their perceptive abilities to solve challenges by recalling visual details. The goal is to emphasize creativity, clarity, logic, and memory, testing your ability to notice and recall the smallest details.

Creative Observation

Showcase your ability to notice and recall visual details through carefully crafted challenges.

Team Collaboration

Work in teams of two to solve challenging memory problems through effective collaboration.

Amazing Prizes

Compete for a prize pool of ₹10,000 and showcase your observation and memory skills.

Rules & Guidelines

General Rules

  • All participants must register before the deadline to be eligible for participation.
  • Participants must arrive at least 30 minutes before their scheduled activity time.
  • Valid photo identification is required for verification at the venue.
  • Use of electronic devices during activities is strictly prohibited unless specifically permitted.
  • The decision of the judges will be final and binding.

Participation Guidelines

  • Follow all instructions provided by event coordinators.
  • Maintain a respectful environment for all participants.
  • Adhere to the time limits for each activity.
  • Any form of cheating or unfair practice will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Participants are responsible for their personal belongings.

Scoring Criteria

  • Accuracy of observations
  • Speed of recall
  • Attention to detail
  • Problem-solving abilities
  • Creativity in approaches (where applicable)
