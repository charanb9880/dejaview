About the Event

Deja View is an exciting inter-college web development competition where teams are tasked with recreating a website presented to them at the event. Participants will leverage their frontend skills to redesign the site while retaining its core features and functionality. The challenge encourages creativity and technical proficiency, allowing teams to showcase their unique approaches to layout, design, and user interaction. Final submissions will be judged based on how closely they align with the original website’s concept, code quality, visual appeal, user-friendliness, and any innovative enhancements. This event fosters collaboration and inspires budding web developers to push the boundaries of their creativity and technical abilities.

Rules & Guidelines

  • Each team can have a minimum of 1 and maximum of 2 participants.
  • Participants must bring their own laptops and chargers.
  • Internet access will be provided during the event.
  • The reference website will be displayed only once, so pay close attention.
  • Teams will be judged on accuracy, creativity, code quality, responsiveness, and user interface.
  • Use of any frameworks or libraries (like Bootstrap, Tailwind, etc.) is allowed.
  • Plagiarism or copying from other teams is strictly prohibited and may lead to disqualification.
  • Decisions made by the judging panel will be final and binding.
  • Judging Criteria: Design & Aesthetics 30%, Functionality 30%, Innovation & Creativity 20%, Code Quality & Structure 10% Responsiveness/User Experience 10%
